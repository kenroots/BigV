Dear Director General and the Kenya Wildlife Service Leadership Team,

It is with great enthusiasm that we introduce BigV Safari — an AI-powered wildlife detection platform designed specifically to support conservation bodies, safari tourism operators, and frontline rangers across Kenya's national parks and conservancies.

Kenya's wildlife is among the most extraordinary on the planet, yet the challenges facing those who protect it are growing: manual patrol limitations, delayed incident reporting, incomplete population tracking, and insufficient early-warning systems for both tourists and rangers. BigV Safari was built to address precisely these gaps — bringing real-time, AI-driven intelligence directly to the field via any smartphone.
